## January Sambrook

Data analyst. Maths teacher and Edexcel A Level examiner, and before that a transport
planner and modeller, which was data work under another name: cleaning inconsistent
source files, running models in batch, and analysing the output to calibrate and
validate against observed data.

I build in **Power BI**, **DAX**, **Power Query**, **SQL**, **Python** and **Excel**.
What I care about in an analysis is whether the number survives being checked, and
whether the limitations are stated where the reader will see them rather than buried.

### Projects

**[West Yorkshire Bus Performance](https://github.com/januarysambrook/West-Yorkshire-Bus-Performance)**
· Power BI · DAX · Power Query
Post-pandemic bus recovery, built from four DfT statistical tables and benchmarked
against five peer combined authorities. Passenger journeys are back to 81% of 2019 but
vehicle miles only to 75%, and the mileage that survived is increasingly publicly
supported.
[Live report](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiMWI1ZDc5NzQtYzAwZi00Nzk2LThjNTgtMTdiY2ZkN2JlNGMxIiwidCI6IjBkM2I2MTRlLTQ0YzEtNDg3Zi05N2YzLWVmZjhiM2Y5NzE3YiJ9)

**[Novastream Churn Analysis](https://github.com/januarysambrook/Novastream-Churn-Analysis)**
· Power BI · DAX
Customer churn from raw subscription tables. Dormant customers churn at 75% against an
overall 23.9%, while region, payment method and gender do not move churn at all.
Cleaning moved the headline rate by a full percentage point.

**[Northstar Tutoring Analytics](https://github.com/januarysambrook/Northstar-Tutoring-Analysis)**
· Power BI · DAX
Star schema and measure library over 520 tutoring sessions. Tutor cost was not in the
data, so it is derived row by row with `SUMX` and `RELATED`. 29.8% gross margin, with
83% of revenue concentrated in one subject family.

**[World Happiness 2019](https://github.com/januarysambrook/World-Happiness)**
· SQL
Thirteen queries moving from description to ranked comparison, using window functions in
CTEs to find where GDP rank and happiness rank come apart.

**[Bookstore Performance](https://github.com/januarysambrook/bookstore-performance-tableau)**
· Tableau
Sales, profitability and trend analysis.
